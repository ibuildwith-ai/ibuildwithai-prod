Privacy Policy

Last updated September 15, 2026

This policy explains how Red Pill, Blue Pill Studios, LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”) handles privacy on this website, www.ibuildwith.ai.

The short version

This is a static website with no user accounts and no advertising. We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site, and you can opt out of it. When you fill in one of our forms, we use what you send only to reply to you, send you our newsletter, or tell you about a workshop. We do not sell or share your personal information.

Information you give us

The site has three forms. Each one sends only the fields you fill in.

  • Contact form: your first and last name, email address, the reason for contacting us, and your message. The email we receive also includes the time, your IP address, and your browser’s user agent, which helps us spot spam.
  • Newsletter signup: your first and last name and email address. We add you to our newsletter list and send ourselves a notification that you signed up. Every newsletter includes a link to unsubscribe.
  • Workshop “Notify Me” and “Join the Waitlist”: your first and last name and email address, plus the workshop you’re interested in. We use it only to contact you about that workshop.

We keep this information in our email and newsletter tools for as long as we need it to reply to you or send you the newsletter. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see Contact).

Service providers that process form data

Our forms are handled by a few trusted service providers that process the data only on our behalf:

  • A serverless hosting provider runs the small functions that receive form submissions. They briefly use your IP address to limit repeated submissions, and the provider may keep standard operational logs.
  • An email delivery service delivers the notification emails our forms send to us.
  • A newsletter platform stores our newsletter list and sends the newsletter.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and improve the site. It collects information such as pages viewed, session statistics, approximate location, and browser and device information. Google processes this information to provide analytics reports to us.

We have turned off Google’s advertising features (Google signals and ad personalization) for this site, and we do not send names, email addresses, or other information that directly identifies you to Google Analytics.

To opt out, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser or extension that blocks analytics scripts.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and review the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies, including _ga and a property-specific _ga_... cookie, to distinguish visitors and sessions. The site sets no other cookies. You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Hosting and server logs

This website is served by a third-party static hosting provider. Like most web hosts, its servers may automatically record standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) in security and operational logs when pages are requested. That processing is handled by the provider under its own privacy practices; we do not control it and do not receive that data.

Fonts and other third parties

This site loads its font from a third-party font service. To display it, your browser requests the font from that service’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser information as part of that request.

Workshop registration and payment happen on third-party sites, such as the webinar host’s registration page or a hosted checkout page. When you follow one of those links, that provider’s privacy policy applies to what you share with them.

Some workshop pages have a teaser video hosted on a third-party video platform. The video doesn’t load until you press play; when you do, the platform’s player loads in privacy-enhanced mode and that platform’s privacy policy applies to your viewing.

Apart from Google Analytics, the font service, and teaser videos you choose to play, we do not embed third-party advertising or social tracking scripts. Links to our social profiles are plain links.

Your choices

  • Analytics: opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser (see Analytics above).
  • Newsletter: unsubscribe with the link in any newsletter, or email us.
  • Your information: email us to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

Children’s privacy

This website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with a new “last updated” date. Material changes will appear on this page before taking effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests about your information? Email us at marcelo@redpillbluepillstudios.com.
