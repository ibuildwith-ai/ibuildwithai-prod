About Marcelo

Claude Certified · Founder · Principal Agentic Builder

Software creation is changing fast. AI now gives knowledge workers and domain experts a new kind of leverage. If you understand your users, your workflows, and your business constraints, you can turn that understanding into prototypes, apps, tools, and automations by collaborating with AI. The shift is not that everyone must become a developer. The shift is that domain expertise plus AI is the new baseline.

I have spent over 30 years building and shipping products across multiple technology shifts, including the personal computer, internet, and mobile eras. I have founded several startups and worked across a wide range of roles at companies including The Cigna Group, ServiceTitan, J.F. Shea, Walt Disney Imagineering, NBC Universal, and Toyota.

Each of those eras changed who gets to build and how ideas move from concept to reality. Now, in the AI era, it's happening again, and faster than ever. That's why I created iBuildWith.ai: to help domain experts apply their expertise with AI in a structured way, so what they build stays useful, understandable, production-ready, and easier to evolve over time.

My domain expertise is content architecture, content modeling, headless CMS, and product development. I'm a Contentful Certified Content Manager and Contentful Certified Professional. I also founded Headless Creator, a learning platform and community for content modeling and headless CMS technologies, which was acquired by Uniform Systems in 2022.

My AI expertise is in agentic development: working with AI coding agents like Claude Code to plan, build, and ship real software. I'm a Claude Certified Associate, and I created Cody Product Builder and Cody Article Writer, agent skills that help domain experts turn ideas into buildable plans and finished work with AI.

Today, at Cigna, as a Principal Agentic Builder, I combine my domain expertise with my AI expertise to create agentic workflows, prototypes, apps, tools, and proofs of concept that help enterprise teams move from ideas to working systems.
