What should I already know?
  • You have used an AI coding tool at least once. Any tool is fine.
  • You can read code, even if you do not write much of it.
How do I prepare?
  • Claude Code installed and working. Run it once beforehand to confirm your setup.
  • One idea to work the process against. A sentence is plenty.
What do I need to watch this?

The recording streams in your browser, on your own schedule.

  • An internet connection — Needed to stream the recording. A normal connection is fine.
  • A web browser — The recording plays in your browser — nothing to install.
  • Speakers or headphones — So you can hear the session.
Presented by

Marcelo Lewin, Founder @ iBuildWith.ai

Marcelo is the founder of iBuildWith.ai and a Principal Content Architect and Builder at Cigna. He has over 30 years of experience in the tech industry. Having lived through the computer, internet, and mobile revolutions, he is now excited and focused on navigating the AI revolution by helping non-developers build real applications using AI.
